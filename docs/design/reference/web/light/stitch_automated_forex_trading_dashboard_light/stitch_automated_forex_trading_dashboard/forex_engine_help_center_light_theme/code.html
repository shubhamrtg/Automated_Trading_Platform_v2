Institutional Trading Glossary

11 Defined Standards

Equity

Balance + Float

Total current net account worth. Equals your settled account balance combined with real-time floating P&L from all open positions.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Starting with $10,000 balance + +$482.10 ▲ floating profit = $10,482.10 total equity.

P&L (Profit & Loss)

Delta Metric

The quantitative net financial gain or loss realized from closed trades or floating across live market orders.

STRICT COLOR CODING
+$42.10 ▲ (Gain) -$18.20 ▼ (Loss) $0.00 — (Even)

Drawdown

Risk Metric

The peak-to-trough decline in account value before a new peak is reached. Measures downside volatility and maximum risk exposure.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

If an account dips from $10,000 to $9,380, the measured drawdown is precisely -6.20% ▼.

Stop Loss (SL)

Order Type

An automated safety order placed with the liquidity provider that terminates a trade at a predetermined price to cap maximum allowable loss.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Long XAUUSD at 2,345.10 with SL at 2,331.40 (-13.70 pts max risk boundary).

Take Profit (TP)

Order Type

An automated order that liquidates a trade when a target favorable price level is reached, securing profits into settled cash balance.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Long XAUUSD at 2,345.10 with TP target at 2,366.00 (+20.90 pts ▲ locked profit).

Spread

Execution Cost

The difference between the broker’s buy (Ask) and sell (Bid) price. High spreads during volatile news events are auto-avoided by the engine.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

EURUSD Ask 1.0846 vs Bid 1.0845 = 1.0 pip spread. AuraFX blocks entry if > 1.8 pips.

Slippage

Telemetry

The variance between requested algorithmic trigger price and the actual fill price received from the broker’s interbank liquidity stream.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Expected fill at 2,345.08, actual fill 2,345.10 = +0.02 pip slippage (within normal 14ms bound).

Backtest

Simulation

Simulating how an algorithmic trading strategy would have executed historically using past market price ticks, variable spreads, and liquidity models.

OPERATIONAL RULE

Must span 5+ continuous years across high-volatility macro regimes before progressing to forward paper tests.

Paper Trading

Sandbox

Simulating live orders in real-time streaming market prices without committing real fiat capital. Tests latency, order queues, and fills.

SANDBOX STATUS

AuraFX executes all new strategies in isolated Paper Mode until manual operator elevation.

Forward Test

Out-of-Sample

An out-of-sample live paper evaluation over an extended period (strictly 30 continuous calendar days) to confirm that a strategy wasn’t simply curve-fit.

BENCHMARK CRITERIA

Hurdle: Maximum Drawdown < 5.0% and Profit Factor ≥ 1.40 over 30 unbroken sessions.

Kill Switch

Emergency Breaker

An emergency one-click master circuit breaker that liquidates all open market positions immediately via rapid market orders and severs broker order connectivity.

RESPONSE LATENCY

Dispatches synchronous IOC cancellation sweeps across FIX/WebSocket endpoints in < 18ms.

Security & Operations

Frequently Answered Protocols

check_circle Institutional Guardrails Active

Yes. AuraFX is built upon strict institutional segregation standards:

  • Asset Segregation: Client trading capital is custodied exclusively at Tier-1 regulated prime brokerages (FCA / ASIC / CFTC registered). AuraFX never holds or commingles your fiat or crypto balance.
  • Read-Only Copilot Scopes: The embedded AI Assistant and telemetry engines are locked to strict read-only permissions and are architecturally prohibited from triggering order dispatches.
  • Hardware Key Authorization: Live order routing requires physical WebAuthn/FIDO2 hardware key verification directly from your desktop browser session.
  • Automated Account-Level Breakers: Hard circuit breakers instantly pause bot activity if daily drawdowns touch pre-set risk limits (default 3.0%), eliminating rogue algorithmic losses.

When pressed, the red KILL SWITCH initiates an immediate master fail-safe routine:

  1. Dispatches synchronous IOC (Immediate-Or-Cancel) market orders to liquidate all active positions across gold (XAUUSD), forex majors, and indices in parallel.
  2. Cancels all pending Limit, Stop, and Trailing orders resting on broker order books.
  3. Revokes strategy execution authority, severing automated order streams until an authorized operator manually resets the system state.

Backtesting is the quantitative foundation of algorithmic discipline. Testing across historical data ensures:

  • Regime Robustness: Proves whether your logic performs consistently across trending markets, quiet consolidation zones, and volatile interest rate decision spikes.
  • Overfitting Prevention: AuraFX flags strategies with suspiciously elevated win rates (>85%) that may be curve-fitted to past noise rather than structural market realities.
  • Psychological & Capital Safety: Visualizing historical drawdowns (e.g. knowing a strategy experienced a -4.20% ▼ dip in 2022) prepares you for real-world statistical variance without knee-jerk manual interventions.
